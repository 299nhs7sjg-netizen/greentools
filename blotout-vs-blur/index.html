Blur / pixelate vs permanent burn

  • Typical screenshot blur — softens or pixelates a region. Looks safe in Slack or a tweet, but the data often remains recoverable with deblur / upsample tricks.
  • Overlay “black boxes” — a layer sitting on top of the image can be stripped or ignored when someone re-exports or screenshots again.
  • BlotOut solid burn — fills the region with opaque black in the exported pixels. What you black out is what people get. Nothing to peel.

The problem this page is about

People redact screenshots for support tickets, changelogs, and social posts every day. Choosing blur because it’s the default tool in an editor is a common mistake: the secret is still in the file. If the content is sensitive enough to hide, it should be destroyed in the export — not disguised.
